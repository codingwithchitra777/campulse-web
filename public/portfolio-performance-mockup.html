💡 UX/UI Recommendation: Interactive Performance Timeline

Instead of simple static lists, we recommend a dual-axis timeline chart using **Chart.js** built-in gradients. The **Invested Capital (Principal)** line remains as a stable anchor representing money actually spent buying positions, while the **Portfolio Market Value (Equity)** line fluctuates in real-time based on active stock quotes. Shading the area between the lines creates an intuitive visual indicator of active/unrealized profit (green glow) and loss (red glow).
